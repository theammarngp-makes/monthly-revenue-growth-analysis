# Presentation Notes — Revenue Intelligence

Short-form talking points, structured for a 5-10 minute stakeholder
walkthrough of the dashboard. Full detail lives in
`reports/executive_report.md`.

## Slide 1 — The question
"Are we growing, and how fast? Where should we look closer?"

## Slide 2 — The headline
- 15,737,501 total revenue across 23 months (Oct-2016 - Aug-2018).
- Ramp phase through Nov-2017, plateau since roughly Apr-2018.
- No sustained decline anywhere in the data.

## Slide 3 — The trend (show `assets/revenue-trend.png` / Tableau line chart)
- Peak month: May-2018 (1,061,000).
- Nov-2017 spike (1,027,013) followed by the sharpest pullback in the
  series (Dec-2017, -24.05%) — a sharp spike and its reversion, not a
  new baseline. The data doesn't say what drove it.

## Slide 4 — Is the recent dip a problem?
- Aug-2018 was -4.00% MoM — but July was +0.53%. That's a 1-month streak,
  not a trend. The data has never shown a 2+ consecutive month decline.
- Last 3 months vs. prior 3 months: -1.59%, within normal volatility.

## Slide 5 — What we can't answer yet
- Whether the plateau is about order volume, order value, or something
  else entirely — we don't have order-level data. That's the single
  biggest gap to close next.

## Slide 6 — Recommendations
1. Rolling-average + streak view instead of single-month headlines.
2. Investigate the Nov-2017 spike to determine whether it is repeatable.
3. Alert on 2+ consecutive decline months (not yet triggered).
4. Get order-level data to unlock the volume-vs-value question.
5. Reconcile against a second revenue source before finance sign-off.
