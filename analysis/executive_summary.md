# Executive Summary

**Period covered:** October 2016 - August 2018 (23 months)
**Total revenue recognized:** 15,737,501
**Scope:** Revenue growth analysis only — order volume and AOV are out of
scope for this engagement (see `docs/assumptions.md`).

## Headline

Revenue grew from 43,000 in the first observed month to a peak of
1,061,000 (May-2018), a launch-to-maturity ramp. The most recent 3 months
on record (Jun-Aug 2018) averaged 1,022,829 versus 1,039,333 for the prior
3 months — a **-1.59% change**: essentially flat, not a meaningful
deterioration.

## What the data shows

1. Revenue rose steeply from Oct-2016 through a Nov-2017 peak
   (1,027,013), then plateaued in a ~860,000-1,061,000 band through
   Aug-2018.
2. There is **no multi-month consecutive revenue decline** anywhere in
   the 23-month series — the longest decline streak is a single month.
   The most recent month (Aug-2018, -4.00%) follows a positive July
   (+0.53%), so it reads as an isolated dip, not the start of a trend.
3. Data quality is clean: no nulls, no duplicates, no gaps, no invalid
   values (`docs/data_quality.md`).
4. Coverage is limited: only 11 of 23 months support a Year-over-Year
   comparison, and the earliest MoM growth percentages reflect a
   near-zero starting base rather than a repeatable growth rate
   (`docs/metric_definitions.md`).

## What this data cannot answer

Whether the plateau is caused by demand saturation, order volume vs.
order value shifts, seasonality, or capacity — this dataset has no order
count, customer data, or external event data to support that
decomposition. See `docs/assumptions.md`.
